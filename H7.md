<h1>7 Things You Should Know About Tor” by Cooper Quintin:</h1>

<h2>Tor still works:</h2> According to leaked documents by the National Security Agency, Tor has not been cryptographically broken. Browsers, user errors, and correlation of traffic are the most effective methods of attack, rather than Tor.

<h2>Not only for criminals:</h2>Tor is used by a wide range of people that have legitimate needs like activists, journalists, families, researchers, and the military to maintain their privacy or to evade censorship.

<h2>No military backdoor:</h2> Tor is open-source and publicly audited and no backdoor has been discovered, even though it was originally funded by the United Navy.

<h2>Running a Tor relay is not illegal in US:</h2> It is not an illegal activity to run a Tor relay in the U.S.: The Electronic Frontier Foundation states that no U.S. citizen has been prosecuted by merely operating a Tor relay, although exit relay can draw the attention of the law-enforcement.

<h2>Tor is user-friendly:</h2> Tor can be used safely with the help of tools such as Tor Browser or Tails.

<h2>Speed of tor has been enhanced:</h2> It is not as fast as usual internet but the network is faster than ever. Increased relays facilitate speed.

<h2>Tor is not fullproff:</h2> Tor may be abused by users to lose their anonymity (e.g. log in personal accounts). Traffic analysis can identify the user in case a person monitors both sides of a connection.

<h2>Overall Message:</h2> Tor is an effective privacy tool, however, it should be utilized properly, and it is not fully anonymous.

The chapter describes how Tor (The Onion Router) facilitates the use of anonymous communication in the internet.
Tor enables regular people conceal their identity and their location as they navigate or interact over the Internet.
Tor can make it extremely challenging to find the identity of the user, but even with digital forensic investigations, it can still happen that some artifacts exist on the devices used to access Tor.
With this, investigators consider evidence that is deposited on the computers instead of attempting to directly trace Tor traffic. ([O'Reilly Media][1])

<h2>History and Intended Use of The Onion Router.</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Tor builds on the onion routing designed in the mid-1990s by the U.S. Naval Research Laboratory by other researchers such as Paul Syverson, Michael G. Reed and David Goldschlag.</li>
  <li>The technology was initially aimed at shielding the communications of the government and intelligence against surveillance.</li>
  <li>It was subsequently publicized and expanded on to Tor to enable every person to talk anonymously over the Internet.</li>
  <li>Tor is not used without a purpose: it is used to preserve privacy, avoid censorship, and provide safe communications.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How The Onion Router Works</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Tor directs internet traffic by using several relays that are run by volunteers all over the world.</li>
  <li>The information is encrypted by several layers of protection, such as layers of an onion.</li>
  <li>The individual relays decrypt one layer, leaving the next destination in the route.</li>
  <li>Each relay does not know the source and the destination of the traffic.</li>
  <li>This design prevents the real IP address of the user to be traced in the least.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Tor and TOR Tracking Criminals.</h2>

Tor has the capability of concealing identities on the Internet, yet it is possible to conduct investigations concerning criminals who use Tor.
The investigator may need to consider:
<ul>
  <li>Evidence on the recovered devices in digital form.</li>
  <li>Suspects are involved in operational errors.</li>
  <li>Associating activity between services or accounts.</li>
  <li>Although Tor may conceal the activities on the network, information left behind on computers, logs or other services can be used to identify suspects.</li>
  <li>The police thus depend on conventional methods of investigations and digital forensics.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Overall:</h2>
Tor offers very good anonymity with layered encryption and relay routing, but is not flawless the investigators still can discover criminals using forensic evidence, errors or off the Tor network.
